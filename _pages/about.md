# Welcome!

I am a postdoctoral research scholar at Department of Marine, Earth and Atmospheric Sciences, North Carolina State University. My work forcuses on integrating geological observations with geomorphological and topographic analysis. I also utilize quantitative analysis including low-temperature thermochronology (e.g. 4He/3He, AHe, AFT and ZHe), and cosmogenic nuclide dating to investigate the impacts of tectonic and climate changes in different landscape settings, including Kyrgyz Tian Shan, eastern Tibet and SE Australia. Please see my [Research](/research/index.html) page for details of past and current projects.

## Research Interests

* Quantifying the contribution from uplifting/tilting to valley-scale vertical erosion 
* Intergrating Thermo-kinematic modelling with Landscape evolution modelling 
* Study the formation process and drivers of low-relief surfaces from mountain belts
